Chapter 01Before

They met in the ordinary way: under a sky that had forgotten how to rain, with two cups of coffee cooling between them.

There was no thunder. No music, no impossible sign from the universe. Only the soft recognition of a familiar stranger, the feeling that a door had opened somewhere neither of them could see.

They spoke until the cafe turned its chairs upside down. When they left, the city looked newly made. Even the red light seemed to wait for them.

Every beginning is a kind of promise.—

What if the heart can recognize home
before the life is ready to hold it?

Chapter 02Almost
They kept
missing by
minutes.

She waited on Tuesday. He arrived on Wednesday.

He called when her phone was face down in a quiet room. She wrote the message, read it twice, and saved it for a braver version of herself.

Their love became a collection of near encounters: a train leaving as the other reached the platform, a letter posted one morning after the address had changed, a photograph taken from opposite sides of the same window.

“We were never far apart. We were only never there at the same time.”

Chapter 03After

Years later, they learned to tell the story without saying each other’s names.

It was easier that way. Names make a story feel finished, and theirs had never learned the shape of an ending.

Sometimes, walking home, she would see his kind of coat in the distance. Sometimes, he would hear her laugh in a crowded room. The body remembers what the mind has carefully put away.

They did not become strangers. Strangers are clean. They became a small ache with excellent manners.

Chapter 04Still

The last page is not a goodbye

Somewhere,
the timing
is kind.

Maybe that is what remains: not regret, but a tenderness for the lives they almost shared. A quiet hope that, in another arrangement of days, the same two hearts might find the same room.

For the ones who arrived too early,
and the ones who arrived too late.
